Invoice follow-up

Past-due invoice email templates that stay firm and professional.

Use these reminders when a client has not paid yet, a card failed, or an invoice needs one clear next step.

Day 0

Invoice sent

Hi {{first_name}}, invoice {{invoice_number}} for {{amount}} is ready here: {{payment_link}}. Thank you again for working with us.

Day 7

Friendly overdue reminder

Hi {{first_name}}, invoice {{invoice_number}} for {{amount}} is now 7 days open. Please use this link when you have a moment: {{payment_link}}. If there is an issue, reply today so I can fix it.

Day 14

Clear payment deadline

Hi {{first_name}}, following up on invoice {{invoice_number}}. The balance of {{amount}} is still unpaid. Please pay by {{date}} or let me know what is blocking it.

Failed card

Payment did not go through

Hi {{first_name}}, it looks like the payment for invoice {{invoice_number}} did not go through. Please retry here: {{payment_link}}. If you need a different method, reply and I will send options.

Dispute

Client says something is wrong

Hi {{first_name}}, thanks for flagging the invoice issue. Please reply with the line item that looks wrong, and I will review it today so we can close this cleanly.
