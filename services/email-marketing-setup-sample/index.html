Sample deliverable

Email marketing setup and training plan.

This sample shows the format of a handoff for a small brand that needs an email list, templates, a welcome sequence, signup forms, and a simple process the owner can run after delivery.

List structure

One clean audience first.

  • Audience: Website, Facebook, and Instagram subscribers
  • Required fields: first name, email, source, interest, signup date
  • Tags: website-form, facebook, instagram, buyer, parent, teacher, promo-opt-in
  • Segments: new subscribers, warm readers, recent buyers, dormant subscribers

Templates

Reusable message blocks.

  • Newsletter: story update, featured illustration, buying link, social prompt
  • Limited promo: offer, deadline, buying link, short FAQ, reminder line
  • Educational spotlight: book lesson, reading activity, parent/teacher note

Welcome sequence

Four emails for the first ten days.

Email 1: Welcome and promise

Subject: Welcome to the reading list
Thanks for joining. Over the next few days, you will get a quick look at the series, the best starting point, and a few reading ideas you can use at home or in class.

Email 2: Meet the books

Subject: Start with this story
Introduce the first recommended title, who it is for, and why it works. Include one cover image, a short excerpt summary, and one clear buying link.

Email 3: Reading activity

Subject: A simple activity for young readers
Share one classroom or family activity connected to the book. Keep the call to action soft: reply with the reader age, or visit the full book page.

Email 4: Buy or follow

Subject: Ready to choose a book?
Offer two options: buy the recommended title or follow the brand on Facebook/Instagram for previews and future reading ideas.

Forms

Signup copy.

Get reading ideas, new-book updates, and occasional offers for illustrated children's books. Join the list and start with our recommended first story.

Handoff

Owner checklist.

  • Send one newsletter per month
  • Send promo emails only when there is a real offer or deadline
  • Check open rate, click rate, and replies after each send
  • Keep source tags clean so future campaigns can be compared
