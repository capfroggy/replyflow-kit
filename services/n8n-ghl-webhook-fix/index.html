First checks

When a GHL custom field is missing.

  • Confirm the field exists on the correct object, usually Contact, not Opportunity or a nested form-only field.
  • Confirm the workflow step is using the exact custom field key or merge tag, not just the display label.
  • Use a dummy contact or booking to test the trigger context before changing active automations.
