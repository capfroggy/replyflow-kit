# ReplyFlow Kit Free Sample

Use these scripts in email, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Facebook Messenger, or
your invoice tool. Replace the fields inside `{{ }}` before sending.

## New Lead

Hi {{first_name}}, thanks for reaching out about {{service}}. I can help. What
is the best time today to ask two quick questions and give you a clear next
step?

## Quote Follow-up

Hi {{first_name}}, I wanted to make sure the quote for {{service}} came through.
The fastest way forward is {{next_step}}. Want me to hold a spot for
{{date_option}}?

## Invoice Reminder

Hi {{first_name}}, invoice {{invoice_number}} for {{amount}} is now 7 days open.
Please use this link when you have a moment: {{payment_link}}. If there is an
issue, reply today so I can fix it.

## WhatsApp Price Reply

{{detalle}}. Con eso te digo si es algo sencillo o si requiere revisar mas
datos.
